Expiry & waste control

Identify expired products and batches approaching their expiry date.

Stock safety workflow: use FEFO (First Expiry, First Out): among suitable, non-quarantined batches, dispense the batch that expires soonest. Inspect storage conditions, packaging, batch numbers and expiry dates at receiving and during routine stock checks. Immediately quarantine expired, damaged, recalled or suspect products so they cannot be sold. Record every return, write-off and disposal with the quantity, reason, date and responsible staff member, following your pharmacy's approved procedure and local regulations. Prototype limitation: expiry alerts use this device's date/time; use a reliable server clock in production.
