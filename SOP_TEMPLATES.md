# MP PHARMAI standard operating procedure (SOP) templates

These are controlled-document outlines, not approved SOPs. The responsible pharmacist and management must adapt each one to current Rwanda law, product classes, actual premises and approved clinical guidance before staff use. Add document number, version, effective date, approver, review date and staff training record to every SOP.

## SOP-01 Procurement and supplier qualification
**Purpose:** procure quality-assured products from legitimate sources.  
**Minimum steps:** maintain approved supplier list; verify supplier licensing/authorization; verify product registration/market authorization where applicable; issue purchase order; retain invoice and delivery records; investigate suspect/unauthorized products; review supplier performance.

## SOP-02 Goods receiving and batch entry
**Purpose:** ensure delivered stock matches order and remains traceable.  
**Minimum steps:** check vehicle/transport condition; product identity, strength, form, pack and quantity; packaging integrity; manufacturer batch/lot; expiry; storage/cold-chain records; supplier and invoice; reject or quarantine discrepancies; enter each batch separately; second-person check for high-risk categories.

## SOP-03 Storage and temperature monitoring
**Purpose:** preserve product quality.  
**Minimum steps:** follow labelled storage conditions; segregate categories; monitor and record room/refrigerator temperatures at approved frequency; calibrate equipment; document excursions; quarantine affected stock pending pharmacist assessment; maintain pest control, cleanliness and security.

## SOP-04 Stock rotation, counts and adjustments
**Purpose:** maintain accurate balances.  
**Minimum steps:** FEFO rotation; scheduled cycle counts; independent recount of discrepancies; adjustments require reason and authorization; retain movement evidence; investigate repeated variances; never erase movement history.

## SOP-05 Prescription assessment and dispensing
**Purpose:** ensure safe and lawful supply.  
**Minimum steps:** confirm valid prescription where required; patient identity and relevant clinical information; product, dose, route, duration and quantity; allergy/interaction/contraindication review within professional scope; clarify unclear orders; pharmacist intervention where required; label, counsel, document and refer when needed. Follow current national clinical and antimicrobial guidance.

## SOP-06 Quarantine, recall and suspect products
**Purpose:** prevent supply of unsafe or recalled products.  
**Minimum steps:** stop supply; identify and physically segregate all affected batches; electronically block; notify responsible pharmacist/management; follow regulator/supplier notice; trace affected stock and transactions; communicate as required; document return/disposal and closure.

## SOP-07 Returns, damaged stock and waste
**Purpose:** ensure returned/damaged/expired stock is not resold improperly.  
**Minimum steps:** assess return eligibility; do not return to saleable stock without authorized documented assessment; quarantine; record quantity, reason and reference; follow authorized supplier return or licensed waste route; retain destruction/transfer evidence.

## SOP-08 Complaints and medication incidents
**Purpose:** manage quality and patient-safety concerns.  
**Minimum steps:** receive and log complaint; protect patient; escalate immediately to responsible pharmacist; preserve product/packaging and batch details; assess reporting obligations; investigate root cause; implement corrective/preventive actions; close with documented review.

## SOP-09 Controlled / restricted medicines
**Purpose:** comply with category-specific controls.  
**Minimum steps:** only implement after verifying current law and licence conditions; authorized personnel only; secure storage; legally required registers and prescriptions; independent counts; discrepancy escalation; approved destruction/returns; periodic reconciliation.

## SOP-10 System access, privacy and downtime
**Purpose:** protect information and maintain continuity.  
**Minimum steps:** individual accounts; least privilege; prompt leaver access removal; no password sharing; screen locking; approved devices; no patient data in demo/localStorage screens; report suspected breach promptly; use approved downtime records; reconcile downtime transactions after restoration; verify backup restore periodically.
